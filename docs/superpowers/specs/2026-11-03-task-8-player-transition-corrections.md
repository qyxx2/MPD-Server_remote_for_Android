# Task 8 Player Transition Corrections — Behavioral Supplement

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Branch:** feature/task-8-player-interaction-polish
**Applies to:** Task 8 Batch 4 artwork/mini-player transition only. This supplement corrects the interaction details observed after device installation; it does not authorize Batch 5 popup work.

## Required behavior

1. **Mini-player pull-up remains pointer-driven until release.** While the pointer is pressed, the extracted Player sheet must remain at the progress derived from the current drag distance. Crossing the 50%-of-window commit threshold or reaching the fling-velocity threshold must not navigate, start the 250ms settle, or stop following the finger before release. On pointer release, apply the existing distance/velocity commit policy: commit and settle to full screen when eligible; otherwise restore the mini-player. Direct tap remains unchanged.
2. **Dismissal backdrop is edge-to-edge.** During downward dismissal, the root dark scrim must cover the complete app window canvas, including the status-bar inset and navigation-bar inset. Foreground root content may keep safe-inset padding; the scrim must not inherit that padding. Scrim opacity continues to follow existing dismissal progress.
3. **Entry artwork ownership has no one-frame reappearance, including preview-to-route commit.** Once an identity-matching mini-player artwork and source rectangle are known and expansion starts, the transition overlay must render at the exact source rectangle even while the full-screen target rectangle is not yet measured. The stationary mini-player image remains hidden while the overlay owns it. When the destination measurement becomes available, the overlay begins interpolating toward it without a frame jump. Once the finger-following preview has measured the full-screen destination, preserve that measured rectangle when pointer-up commits the FULL_SCREEN_PLAYER route; do not clear it during the handoff. Clearing the target makes the overlay use its documented null-target fallback (`target = source`), which jumps the image back to the mini-player position while both base images are hidden, then jumps it forward when the new route reports bounds. It is safe to clear this measurement at the start of a new pull-up preview or a direct-tap open, before a new destination is measured. Never use a stale artwork cache key.
4. **Hidden full-screen artwork does not leave a fixed placeholder.** When the full-screen ArtworkStage is hidden for a shared-element handoff, its background/surface placeholder must also be hidden. The transition overlay is the sole visible owner of the cover image.
5. **Dismissal artwork follows the Player sheet and then lands linearly on the mini-player.** During a downward Player dismissal, an identity-checked overlay moves the current artwork from the stable full-screen ArtworkStage rectangle to the measured mini-player artwork rectangle. Capture and freeze the full-screen source rectangle before applying the first Player drag translation. Do not derive a new source from `positionOnScreen()` during the drag: the child is inside a `graphicsLayer`-translated Player sheet, and the measured callback can remain at its pre-translation position while the layer moves. Derive artwork progress from the measured vertical travel between the frozen source and target artwork top edges: `clamp(playerDragOffsetPx / (target.top - source.top), 0, 1)`. Interpolate all four rectangle edges (left, top, right, bottom) linearly using this one artwork progress. This makes the artwork top move down by exactly the Player sheet's finger-driven translation until it reaches the measured mini-player top; using `playerDragOffsetPx / playerLayerHeightPx` for artwork incorrectly makes the cover lag because the artwork anchors are closer together than the full Player layer. Once the artwork reaches the mini-player, it remains at that measured target while the remaining Player sheet exits. The sheet and backdrop continue to use their own existing raw window-height drag progress. Do not use eased curves or switch trajectory during the final part of the drag. The full-screen stage and stationary mini-player image are hidden while the overlay owns the art. If the mini-player target measurement is not ready, keep the overlay anchored at the full-screen source rectangle until it is measured. At artwork progress 1, land exactly on the measured mini-player bounds and hand off to the normal mini-player image without a visible jump.

## Invariants

- Preserve the existing 50% distance and upward-fling thresholds as *release-time* commit rules.
- Preserve the existing 250ms settle animation after the pointer is released and the gesture is accepted.
- Preserve direct tap behavior and early-release cancellation.
- The artwork overlay consumes only the already-loaded bitmap whose PlayerArtworkCacheKey matches the current confirmed artwork identity. No new loader, request, playback owner or cache owner is introduced.
- Use measured screen/root-coordinate bounds; do not hard-code device dimensions.
- No server, network, queue, database, media-session, navigation-stack, or playback-state authority changes.
- No changes to Task 8 Batch 5 popup/dialog work.
